Featured Research

Context-Aware Root Cause Analysis for Android Automotive OS Logs

Master's thesis conducted in collaboration with Volvo Cars as part of the Artificial Intelligence Engineering programme at Jönköping University.


The research explored how Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and strategic reasoning (CoT) can support explainable and context-aware root cause analysis of Android Automotive OS logs. The work involved designing and evaluating an AI-assisted log analysis framework capable of improving reasoning quality, grounding, and explainability in software diagnostics.
